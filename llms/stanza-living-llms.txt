# Stanza Living

> Stanza Living is one of India's largest managed accommodation and co-living platforms (founded 2017, headquartered in Gurugram). It offers professionally managed, fully furnished, brokerage-free residences — student housing, co-living for working professionals, and managed apartments — across 24+ Indian cities. It operates as a consumer web and mobile brand and does not publish a public developer API.
